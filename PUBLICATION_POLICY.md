# Publication Policy

This repository publishes reusable methods and de-identified examples. It is not a source for live operating data.

## Never publish

- Customer, supplier, or employee identity and contact data
- Actual quotations, costs, margins, purchase prices, payment details, or banking records
- Credentials, tokens, internal hostnames, private prompts, or raw business logs
- Files whose structure or metadata can reasonably reveal a customer or transaction

## What may be published

- Fictional or fully de-identified sample data
- General workflow design, deterministic rules, review gates, tests, and failure-handling patterns
- Screenshots that have been checked for visible and embedded sensitive information

## Before every release

Review examples, configuration, generated files, screenshots, and commit history. If a file cannot be confidently cleared, keep it private.
